### 2.3 Stagger: why the four towers vary, and why R1 is tallest

- **Planning reality.** Chinese planning control asks for 错落 skylines. Four identical 200 m extrusions 66 m apart read as a wall from the river. The user notices things like that.
- **The axis.** R1 is next to 星湾路 and the 星湾桥 bridgehead, the campus's main north–south axis. The tallest tower should mark the entrance. Across the road, A (≈ 180–190 m, codes.md) answers it as a gateway pair. The group then tapers to the campus's east edge (x 286).
- **Structure.** Only R1 comes near the 190 m main-roof limit (186.7 m). R2–R4 have margin.
- **A level datum.** Every tower has its refuge floors at 45.6 / 90.9 / 137.1 m, so the three louvre bands run level across all four and tie the stepped crowns together. The step equals 3 storeys, so the crowns step down in a clean 10.2 m rhythm.
- **Home count.** It stays at 348. The B1 garage and the 1F lobby loads do not grow. That matters because the shared garage already has only 76 stalls (§8.2).
- **Shadow camera.** In the campus view R4's crown is the one most at risk of leaving the shadow frustum. At 169.4 m it is 7.5 m outside; at 200 m it would be 30 m outside (§7.7).

**Alternatives:**
- **All four at 53F / 200 m:** 384 homes (+36), +36 stalls, and the flat-top wall.
- **codes.md's 176 / 188 / 200 / 188:** the peak at R3 has no site reason; it is a good shape but anchors nothing.

### 2.4 RF and crown

- **RF (main roof)** is plant, not a residents' garden. At 157–187 m with wind, a BMU and aviation lights, a public roof garden is not realistic. On RF:
  - the lift machine room over the core (5 m/s traction; codes.md §3.4: overrun 5–7 m, so the room sits at RF + 0…3.5);
  - the roof fire tank ≥ 36 m³ (residential above 100 m, GB 50974 §5.2.1 ≈);

  Stairs ST1 / ST2 and LF reach RF for maintenance and firefighting. Q6 asks the user whether dropping today's 屋顶花园 is acceptable.
- **Crown, 13.3 m, the same on all four:**
  - the façade mullions and the core's stone fins rise above RF as a screen;
  - the slot (§3.2) continues up as a glass seam that splits the crown into two blades, so it reads as **双翼** from P2 and the river;

## 3. Plate and homes

### 3.1 Why the core goes into the 03 wing

- **Structure.** A 200 m tower needs a core of about H/12 in at least one direction (JGJ 3 §9.2.1 ✔). Today's plate has none: a gallery bar, two glass lifts in a light well and two corner stair boxes. The only place 17.6 × 14.3 m fits without moving the GLB homes is the 03 wing.
- **Sun.** 03 faces due north: 0 h on 大寒 (PLAN.md §6 Q8, risk 8). Every home must have a living space that meets the sunlight standard (GB 55038-2025 / GB 50180-2018 ≈). A north-only home cannot be approved.
- **Lifts.** 2 lifts give a 92 s interval today; ~50 home floors need 4 lifts (§4). The 7.6 m light well cannot hold 4 lifts, a fire lift with its 前室, and stairs.
- **The GLB windows decide the rest.** All three prototypes have their entry, kitchen and children's-room windows on the entry (north) wall (`p2_core.mjs`). So:
  - the kitchen window must face open air, which rules out an enclosed corridor running the whole plate;
  - 03 can only take the west slot, because it is not mirrored and would put its kitchen window against the core in the east slot;
  - 02 is the only plan that fits the east slot.
- **Rejected: keep 3 homes per floor with 03 facing north.** Push 03 north and put a hub core between the wings. The 0 h sun stays, the plate grows to about 40.8 × 45 m, and the north home remains unapprovable. A south-facing three-home slab (03 + 01 + 02 side by side) is about 50 m wide. That leaves 14–16 m gaps, and the middle home's kitchen has no window.

## 9. Risks

1. **Three homes per floor become two.**
   - The user asked on 2026-10-09 for the three types "嵌入原 R1–R4，充分使用标准层，并预留双电梯". This design keeps all three types but changes that floor.
   - The home count stays at 348 even though the towers nearly double in height. A user may read that as "taller but not more homes".
   - Present the reasons together: core, sun, lifts, and kitchens that need windows. Offer 384 homes (all 200 m) and "04" (Q2, Q7).
2. **The FPS hash.** R1–R4 are in `buildLayout({expansion:false})`. Two R builders must coexist, and any shared helper edit (`tower()`, `residentialLobby()`, `RESIDENTIAL_PLATE`) breaks `fps-frozen`. Keep the new code in `r-supertall.js`.
3. **Eccentric core** (11 m, 35 % of the depth). It is defensible only with the mega-columns, belts and outriggers drawn and explained. A real 超限 panel would ask for a torsion check.
4. **GLB windows onto the enclosed band** (baths, a laundry, two NE bedrooms). Today they face an open gallery. Draw them frosted. The real answer is fixed obscure glass.
5. **Penthouse quality.** Procedural duplexes sit above GLB homes. The plainer interior is in the most expensive home (Q3).
6. **The render refactor.** Instanced shells with per-instance hiding, per-tower clip materials over shared geometry, and LOD hysteresis around the R1 view distance (570–605 m) may pop. M0 first, measured.
7. **Shared engine work with 北苑:** stair `breakLevels`, ride speed, the lift keypad. Land each once, and do not fork it per tower.
8. **Parking:** 52–76 stalls for 348 luxury homes is unrealistic. B2 helps a little, and a full-plot garage is a bigger project.
9. **R1 B1 arrival re-route** touches tested ramp geometry.
10. **The site's east and south edges:** the four hardstands make the south strip mostly paving. The planting moves to the north strip and the pocket gardens.
11. **Pinned counts move in five tests.** H / A / 北苑 / 西苑 change the same totals (levels, lifts, records, gates). Integrate the deltas in one place (`riverside-integration`) after all designs land.
